# Example: Animation (60s, 9:16)

## Prompt (as received)

"A 60-second vertical 2D animation: a shy stick-figure boy in a red hoodie
meets a girl in the park, they talk, argue, then he walks toward his future.
Same character in every scene, grayscale with red accents, no dialogue text
on screen."

## Production brief (extracted)

- Niche: animation / emotional short. Style: 2D stick-figure, grayscale + red.
- Duration: 60s → 6 clips, 1 batch. Ratio: 9:16 (given).
- Character block (verbatim every clip): white round face, big black eyes, red
  hoodie, black pants, red-and-white sneakers, red-and-black cap worn
  backwards. Girl: black hair in a bun, red top.
- Constraint: no on-screen text — so no subtitles, no captions, no titles in
  the video itself.

## Scene plan

| Clip | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Boy sad at home; thinking; memory bubble of the girl; walks out |
| 2 | Walks alone on the street; meets the girl on a park bench; shy |
| 3 | Bench conversation; he explains his feelings; hands hold |
| 4 | Peaceful moment under the moon; then argument — she is angry, he is hurt |
| 5 | Alone on the bench at night; determined close-up; stands up |
| 6 | Long shot from behind: walks toward the horizon |

## Continuity notes

- Final-frame reference at every boundary; the bench, the park, and the
  moonlit sky block repeat verbatim.
- First generation batch came back in a non-9:16 size: per skill rules it was
  regenerated natively in 720x1280, not stretched, blur-filled, or cropped.
- Deliverable: 60s video + brief + storyboard + continuity log + QC report.
