# Example: Long-Form Video (10 min, 16:9)

## Prompt (as received)

"A 10-minute horizontal video: how a small bakery grew from one oven to a
town favourite. Documentary style, warm narration, interviews with the owner,
morning-to-evening structure."

## Production brief (extracted)

- Niche: documentary / small business. Style: documentary, natural light.
- Duration: 10 min → 60 clips. Ratio: 16:9 (given).
- Language: narration language from prompt (English as supplied); owner
  interview lines preserved verbatim.
- Structure: episodic chapters (Dawn / First Customers / The Rush / Afternoon
  Craft / Evening Regulars / Closing) with a continuous owner thread.
- Batches: 10 batches of 6 clips = 10 user messages. Stated up front.

## Scene plan (abridged)

| Chapter | Timecode | Clips | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dawn | 0:00–1:40 | 1–10 | Baker lights the oven, kneads dough, first trays in |
| First Customers | 1:40–3:20 | 11–20 | Doors open, regulars arrive, owner interview beat |
| The Rush | 3:20–5:00 | 21–30 | Queues, oven rotation, near-miss: a burnt batch |
| Afternoon Craft | 5:00–6:40 | 31–40 | New recipe trial, tasting, decision to add it |
| Evening Regulars | 6:40–8:20 | 41–50 | Families, stories, the shop as community hub |
| Closing | 8:20–10:00 | 51–60 | Lights dim, tomorrow's dough prepared, closing line |

## Continuity discipline

- The bakery interior block (counter, oven position, window light direction)
  repeats verbatim in every interior clip.
- The owner identity block repeats verbatim; apron state (flour marks) is a
  tracked story state, not drift.
- Each chapter boundary is a checkpoint: clips verified, joins inspected,
  continuity log complete before the next chapter starts.

## Delivery honesty

- If clips 21–30 arrive in message 4 and clip 27 fails after 3 attempts, the
  message-4 delivery is a labelled partial batch, and the final film is
  labelled INCOMPLETE until clip 27 is replaced or supplied — never presented
  as the complete 10-minute film.
