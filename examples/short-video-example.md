# Example: Short Video (30s, 9:16)

## Prompt (as received)

"A 30-second vertical video: a lost puppy finds its way home through a rainy
street, guided by a kind stranger. Emotional, realistic smartphone style, no
dialogue, soft piano."

## Production brief (extracted)

- Niche: animal rescue / emotional storytelling. Style: realistic smartphone.
- Duration: 30s → 3 clips. Ratio: 9:16 (given). Dialogue: none (given).
- Language: n/a (no dialogue). Music: soft piano (given).
- Characters: puppy (small brown-and-white spaniel mix, wet fur as story state),
  stranger (woman, yellow raincoat).
- Locations: rainy residential street, front porch.
- Structure: continuous narrative. Hook: puppy alone in rain, first 3 seconds.

## Scene plan

| Clip | Timecode | Content | Batch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–10s | Puppy alone under a shop awning in rain; looks up the street | 1 |
| 2 | 10–20s | Stranger kneels, offers a hand; puppy follows her along the street (final-frame handover: mid-walk) | 1 |
| 3 | 20–30s | Porch reunion: owner opens the door, puppy runs in, tail wagging; gentle piano resolves | 1 |

## Continuity notes

- Clip 1 → 2: rain intensity, wet pavement reflections, puppy's position at
  the awning edge; final frame = puppy stepping out.
- Clip 2 → 3: stranger's yellow raincoat, puppy at her left heel, walking
  direction left-to-right; final frame = porch steps in view.

## QC result (plan)

- All clips measured 10.00s, 720x1280. Joins inspected at 10s and 20s.
- Deliverable: 30s video + brief + storyboard + continuity log + QC report.
