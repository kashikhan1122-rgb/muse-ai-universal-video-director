# Example: Realistic Video (60s, 9:16)

## Prompt (as received)

"A 60-second realistic smartphone video: a street vendor makes fresh orange
juice at dawn, shot like a friend filmed it on a phone. Natural sound, no
music, no captions."

## Production brief (extracted)

- Niche: food / daily life. Style: realistic smartphone footage — handheld
  feel, eye-level framing, natural dawn light. Explicitly NOT cinematic.
- Duration: 60s → 6 clips, 1 batch. Ratio: 9:16.
- Audio: natural sound only (traffic, squeezing, pouring, coins). No music,
  no narration, no captions — all three forbidden by the prompt.
- Character: vendor (man, 50s, apron, rolled sleeves) — identity block
  repeated verbatim; cart block (orange cart, crates, manual press) repeated
  verbatim.

## Scene plan

| Clip | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cart setup at dawn; vendor arranges oranges |
| 2 | First customer; oranges cut and pressed |
| 3 | Close-up: juice pouring, pulp, glass filling |
| 4 | Customer pays, smiles, walks away |
| 5 | Vendor wipes the counter, restocks |
| 6 | Wide: street waking up, vendor serving the next customer |

## Rules applied

- Camera stays handheld and eye-level throughout; no film-style push-ins.
- No music bed added "to improve it" — the prompt forbade music.
- No captions added — the prompt forbade them.
- Natural sound continuity at joins checked: no mid-pour audio cut.
